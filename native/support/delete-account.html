GeneticAge by KoleQuant

Delete your GeneticAge account

You can delete your account here after uninstalling GeneticAge. Use the same sign-in method you chose in the app.

Deletion removes your profile, sign-in information, sessions, saved assessments and reports from the live database. Deleted records can remain in Cloudflare’s recovery backups for up to 30 days. Exported copies stay where you saved them.

Sign in with Apple Sign in with Google

To remove GeneticAge from Sign in with Apple, use your Apple Account settings. Apple’s instructions.

Request help if you can’t sign in

Email KoleQuant with the subject “GeneticAge account deletion” and the sign-in method you used. KoleQuant will verify account ownership before deleting it. Keep health entries, passwords and codes out of your message. Request account deletion by email.

For information about what GeneticAge stores and how it uses it, read the privacy policy.
