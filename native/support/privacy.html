GeneticAge by KoleQuant

Privacy policy

This policy applies to the GeneticAge mobile app and the account services on this website. GeneticAge is for adults aged 18 and older.

Assessments and health information

GeneticAge uses details you enter, such as age, height, weight, activity, medical context and blood results, to calculate assessments and generate educational reports. It derives BMI from height and weight. Unsaved answers and results stay in the app’s memory. When you choose to save, your answers, calculated results and report go to your private account.

GeneticAge encrypts saved health information before it enters the database. HTTPS protects information in transit. KoleQuant uses saved assessments to make your history available when you sign in. The app has no advertising, tracking or research data sharing.

Sign-in and service providers

When you create an account, KoleQuant stores your nickname, account identifier, sign-in information, adult confirmation and storage consent. If you sign in by email, KoleQuant stores the address and Resend delivers your one-time code. Apple and Google handle their own sign-in. KoleQuant stores a protected identifier for each provider account. It stores an encrypted Apple refresh token so that it can revoke Apple access when you delete your account. The sign-in providers receive no health entries.

Cloudflare hosts the account service and database. The service uses short-lived session, sign-in and rate-limit records to secure access and prevent abuse. The app keeps its session credential in memory and saves language and theme preferences on the device.

Export, retention and deletion

KoleQuant keeps account details and saved assessments while you keep your account. Sign-in codes and verification records expire within minutes. Session records expire within 30 days of sign-in. You can export your information or delete your account from Account → Manage saved data in the app.

Deleting your account removes the live profile, sign-in records, active sessions, saved assessments and reports. Deleted records can remain in Cloudflare’s recovery backups for up to 30 days. Copies you exported remain wherever you saved or shared them. You can also request deletion from this website after uninstalling the app. Delete your GeneticAge account.

Contact KoleQuant

For a privacy or data request, email KoleQuant. Include your sign-in method and avoid sending health entries, passwords or codes. support@kolequant.xyz.
