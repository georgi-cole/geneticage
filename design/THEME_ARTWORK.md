Blood Moon v2 preserves the matte copper/garnet palette while refining the lunar geology and edge lighting. Its genuinely transparent breathing room is retained in both WebP delivery sizes. The earlier resource remains for provenance. The other three WebP resources are the selected preview resources without further creative edits.

The distinct Overview/Reports resources have 256 px variants and retained RGBA originals. Current palette/branch edits and exact prompts are recorded in [SILVER_BLUE_AND_MOON.md](SILVER_BLUE_AND_MOON.md); earlier studies and Bionic reference handling remain in [SCREEN_ARTWORK.md](SCREEN_ARTWORK.md). Report covers use contained artwork rather than enlarged document icons or orbit rings. Native text, controls and numerical results stay separate from the artwork. Bionic (formerly Futuristic) also retains its earlier generated specimen for provenance and uses abstract articulated native geometry in Overview/Assessments. Its original resource and prompt are in [BIONIC_ARTWORK.md](BIONIC_ARTWORK.md). Royal Purple, Silverbolt and Moonshine are kept only in the repository archive for future revisions.

Fonts are self-hosted Latin subsets from Google Fonts. Their SIL Open Font Licenses are stored beside them in `public/assets/fonts/`.
